The rules the bot follows
  • Looks at every coin after every 15 minute candle closes, all day and night.
  • Buy: the Supertrend turns to a buy signal while the price is above the DEMA 200 line. Wait for the candle to close, then enter.
  • Short: the exact opposite: Supertrend turns to sell while the price is below the DEMA.
  • Tip 1: if Supertrend already points the right way and the price then crosses the DEMA line, enter then.
  • Stop loss: at the Supertrend line. The trade is sized so a stop hit loses 1.5% of the account, fees included.
  • Exit: no profit target. Leave when Supertrend flips, or at once if the price touches the outer Fibonacci Bollinger Band (tip 2).
  • At most 6% of the account is at risk across all open trades together. A full-size trade risks 1.5%, so about 4 at once. A setup that would push the total past 6% is skipped.
  • No trade may hold more than the whole account (no borrowing).
  • Down 6% in one day (Toronto time): everything closes and the bot rests until the next day.
  • Coins: BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, ADA, LTC, LINK, AVAX, DOT. A coin is skipped if it traded under 5 million USDT in the last 24 hours.
  • Fee assumed: 0.25% on every buy and every sell.
