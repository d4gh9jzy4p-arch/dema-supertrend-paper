The rules the bot follows
  • Looks at every coin after every 15 minute candle closes, all day and night.
  • Buy: the Supertrend turns to a buy signal while the price is above the DEMA 200 line. Wait for the candle to close, then enter.
  • Short: the exact opposite: Supertrend turns to sell while the price is below the DEMA.
  • Tip 1: if Supertrend already points the right way and the price then crosses the DEMA line, enter then.
  • Stop loss: at the Supertrend line. The trade is sized so a stop hit loses 2% of the account, fees included.
  • Exit: no profit target. Leave when Supertrend flips. Tip 2 (exit at the outer Fibonacci Bollinger Band) was used until Nov 5, 2026 and is now OFF: it closed trades right after they opened.
  • At most 12% of the account is at risk across all open trades together. A full-size trade risks 2%, so about 6 at once. A setup that would push the total past 12% is skipped.
  • Paper borrowing is allowed (up to 10 times the account) so every valid setup can be taken. A real spot account could not borrow, so real results would be smaller.
  • Down 6% in one day (Toronto time): everything closes and the bot rests until the next day.
  • Coins: BTC and DOGE. A coin is skipped if it traded under 5 million USDT in the last 24 hours.
  • Fee assumed: 0.25% on every buy and every sell.
  • History: risk per trade was 1.5% until Nov 4, 2026 and is 2% since. Trades before that used 1.5%. The open-risk cap was 6% until Nov 4, 2026 and is 12% since (it was 10% for a few hours that day). Tip 2 (band exit) has been off since Nov 5, 2026; earlier trades that closed at the band are still listed.
