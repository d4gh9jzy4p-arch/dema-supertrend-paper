Paper Trading Bot: 2 hour test account

DEMA 200 + Supertrend (12, 3), 2 hour candles, 10 big coins, 24 hours a day.

This is the 2 hour test account. It trades the same coins as the original account, but on 2 hour candles, with no tip 1, no band exit, no setups whose stop is closer than 1.5% to the price, and half the risk (1% per trade, 6% open at once). A big backtest liked this version best, but the edge was small and could be luck, so this account is the live test.

The rules the bot follows
  • Looks at every coin after every 2 hour candle closes, all day and night.
  • Buy: the Supertrend turns to a buy signal while the price is above the DEMA 200 line. Wait for the candle to close, then enter.
  • Short: the exact opposite: Supertrend turns to sell while the price is below the DEMA.
  • Tip 1: OFF in this account.
  • Stop loss: at the Supertrend line. The trade is sized so a stop hit loses 1% of the account, fees included.
  • Exit: no profit target. Leave when Supertrend flips. The Fibonacci band exit is OFF in this account.
  • At most 6% of the account is at risk across all open trades together. A full-size trade risks 1%, so about 6 at once. A setup that would push the total past 6% is skipped.
  • Paper borrowing is allowed (up to 10 times the account) so every valid setup can be taken. A real spot account could not borrow, so real results would be smaller.
  • Down 6% in one day (Toronto time): everything closes and the bot rests until the next day.
  • Coins: BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, ADA, LTC, LINK, AVAX, NEAR. A coin is skipped if it traded under 5 million USDT in the last 24 hours.
  • A setup is ignored if the stop is closer than 1.5% to the price (fees eat small stops).
  • Fee assumed: 0.25% on every buy and every sell.
